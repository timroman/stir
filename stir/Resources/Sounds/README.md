# Sounds

Every sound here is generated by `tools/synthesize-sounds.py`. Nothing is
licensed, downloaded, or of unknown origin.

    python3 tools/synthesize-sounds.py <outdir>
    afconvert -f m4af -d aac -b 128000 <name>.wav <name>.m4a

Regenerating gives audibly identical files, not byte-identical ones: the
shipped set was built on an older numpy, and the difference lands 60–72 dB
below peak — nothing anybody can hear, but the hashes do not match.

## The six looping beds

`white_noise`, `pink_noise`, `brown_noise`, `fan`, `wind`, `ocean_waves` —
built sample-continuous end-to-start so they repeat gaplessly with no runtime
crossfade. `rain` is generated but not shipped (cut in review).

## The ten wake tones

`gentle_chime`, `soft_bells`, `singing_bowl`, `deep_bowl`, `temple_bells`,
`wind_chimes`, `music_box`, `marimba`, `dawn`, `first_light` — one-shot, and
`ocean_waves` doubles as a wake sound.

What makes a struck sound read as metal, glass or wood is the ratio of its
partials to the fundamental and how fast each one dies: a free-free bar rings at
1, 2.76, 5.40, while a tuned bell rings hum, prime, tierce, quint, nominal — the
minor-third tierce being why a bell sounds solemn where a bar sounds bright.

`gentle_chime` and `soft_bells` replaced two mp3s of unknown origin on
16 September 2026. See `docs/spec/stir.md`, decision 22.
