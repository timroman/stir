stir is an overnight alarm. To test without waiting a night:
1. Complete onboarding (grant microphone; location and backup alarm optional).
2. On the setup screen, set "up by" 3-4 minutes ahead and tap "start the night".
3. White noise may be skipped automatically for a session this short; the app calibrates to room sound for 30 seconds, then listens. Make a sustained noise (e.g. speak near the phone) to trigger the gentle wake, or wait for the "up by" time - the alarm fires regardless.
4. The night screen intentionally shows no clock; the background warms as the wake time approaches. The two rings are the real sun and moon computed from location.
All processing is on-device. The microphone is only evaluated during the wake window and nothing is recorded. On iOS 26+, an AlarmKit backup alarm is scheduled 2 minutes after "up by" and cancelled when the user stops the session.
