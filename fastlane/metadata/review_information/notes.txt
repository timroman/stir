stir is an overnight alarm. To test without waiting a night:
1. Complete onboarding (grant microphone; location and backup alarm optional).
2. On the setup screen, set "up by" 3-4 minutes ahead and tap "start the night".
3. White noise fades in over ~3 seconds and plays while the session runs. The night screen intentionally shows no clock and stays black - the two rings on it are the real sun and moon computed from location.
4. The app calibrates to room sound for 30 seconds once the white noise has faded, then listens. Make a sustained noise (e.g. speak near the phone) to trigger the gentle wake, or wait for the "up by" time - the alarm fires regardless, with light and volume rising together.
All processing is on-device. The microphone is only evaluated during the wake window and nothing is recorded. On iOS 26+, an AlarmKit backup alarm is scheduled 2 minutes after "up by" and cancelled when the user stops the session.
