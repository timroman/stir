sensitivity can set itself now.

settings → wake has a new "auto" option. it starts from whatever you were already using and moves one step at a time, based on whether stir is hearing you or hearing the room. your setting does not change by itself — this update leaves you exactly where you were, and only auto adjusts anything.

what to try: turn on auto and sleep with it for a couple of weeks. settings tells you whether it is still settling or has settled, and roughly where it landed.

the one question: after an alarm, stir may ask once whether that was too sensitive. yes makes it a step less sensitive and stops it climbing back that high. no changes nothing, and it will not ask again unless your room changes.

what to tell me: did it wake you gently, or did the room wake you? did it leave you to your "up by" time more often than before? and if you got the question, did the answer match what you would have chosen by hand?

nothing about this leaves your phone. technical details shows the step, the state, and how many nights it has counted.
